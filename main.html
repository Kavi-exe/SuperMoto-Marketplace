Safety & Terms

Reference pages for buyer safety, marketplace rules, privacy, and support.

Safe Buying Advice

Inspect every vehicle or spare part in person before payment. Match chassis numbers, import documents, service records, and ownership details with the seller.

  • Meet at a showroom, workshop, bank, or another public verified location.
  • Use an independent mechanic for pre-purchase inspection.
  • Avoid advance transfers before you verify the item and seller identity.

Terms & Conditions

CeylonSuperHub is a classifieds platform. Sellers are responsible for accurate listing details, pricing, document status, and availability.

  • Do not post misleading prices, copied listings, or unavailable vehicles.
  • Buyers and sellers must complete their own legal and financial checks.
  • Listings may be removed if they look fraudulent, unsafe, or incomplete.

Privacy Policy

Profile details, listing details, and contact information are stored locally in this demo marketplace experience and shown only where needed for browsing and contact.

  • Only add contact details you are comfortable sharing with buyers.
  • Uploaded profile photos are saved in browser storage for this local site.
  • Clear browser storage if you want to remove locally saved demo data.

Help & Support

Use this area for marketplace assistance, listing edits, buyer reports, and seller verification guidance.

  • For listing changes, update your profile or repost the correct details.
  • Report suspicious prices, duplicate ads, or document mismatches.
  • Add support phone, WhatsApp, and email details here when ready.

Verify Documents

Use this checklist before committing to a high-value vehicle or spare part purchase.

  • Check registration book, customs duty status, revenue license, and insurance.
  • Match seller identity with ownership or authorized sale documents.
  • For imported parts, ask for invoice, part number, and fitment proof.
